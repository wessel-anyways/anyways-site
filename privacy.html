Privacy policy.

Short version: we only collect what we need to answer you, we don't sell it, we don't use tracking cookies, and you can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Who we are

anyways. is a performance marketing agency based in South Africa. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean anyways. We are the responsible party for the personal information described here, under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Our Information Officer is Wessel Breunissen. You can reach us at wessel@anyways.co.za about anything in this policy.

Who handles it for us

We don't sell or rent your information. We use a few service providers to run the site and our business, and they only process it on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail) stores audit requests and visit stats and sends us an email when a request comes in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Cloudflare delivers and protects it. Like any web server, they see your IP address and browser details in their logs, to serve the page and block attacks.
  • Calendly runs the booking calendar on our home page. It handles what you enter there under its own privacy notice.

These providers may store information on servers outside South Africa, mainly in the United States. We only use providers that are bound by data protection laws or agreements that give your information a similar level of protection to POPIA.

Cookies and similar tech

We don't set any cookies, and we don't use advertising or tracking pixels on this site.

  • The anonymous visit stats use a random ID kept in your browser's session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab.
  • If you open the site with ?notrack=1 at the end of the address, your browser remembers (in local storage) not to count your visits. ?notrack=0 undoes it.
  • The Calendly booking calendar on our home page may set its own cookies when it loads. You can block third-party cookies in your browser settings, or email us to book instead.

How long we keep it

  • Audit requests and emails: only as long as we need them to talk to you and, if you become a client, to work with you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Anonymous visit stats: raw visits are deleted after 120 days. We keep daily totals, which contain no personal information.

How we protect it

Your details are kept in private accounts that only we can access, and the site is only served over HTTPS. If we ever have a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct it or delete it;
  • stop using it, or stop contacting you.

Email wessel@anyways.co.za and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with how we handled it, you can complain to the Information Regulator.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
