The usual bad part

  • Spend on searches that never buy. Broad keywords and no negatives, so the budget goes to people looking for jobs, free stuff or a different product.
  • Tracking you can't trust. Conversions counted twice, or not at all, so Google's bidding is optimising for the wrong thing.
  • Performance Max as a black box. A campaign that spends happily on your own brand name and calls it growth.
  • Messy shopping feeds. Products disapproved or showing for the wrong searches because titles and attributes were never cleaned up.

What we do in Google Ads

  • Search campaigns built around buying intent, with tight keyword themes, negatives and ads that match the landing page.
  • Performance Max and Shopping with a cleaned-up Merchant Center feed, brand traffic kept separate and asset groups that make sense.
  • YouTube for reach and remarketing, cut from the same creative that's working on social.
  • Conversion tracking through GA4 and Google Tag Manager, server-side where it matters, so bidding learns from real sales and leads.
  • Landing pages fixed or rebuilt when the page is the reason clicks don't convert.

Questions

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

It depends on what clicks cost in your category and how many sales or leads you need. Your free audit includes a recommended starting budget based on your market. Ad spend is paid by you, straight to Google.

How long before Google Ads works?

Search campaigns can bring buyers from the first day. Performance Max and automated bidding usually need a few weeks of clean conversion data before they settle, which is why we fix tracking first.

Do you run Performance Max and Shopping campaigns?

Yes. We clean up the Merchant Center feed, keep brand searches separate so results aren't flattered, and report on new sales.

Can you take over an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. You keep ownership of the account. We start with an audit of what's there and change what's costing you money first.
